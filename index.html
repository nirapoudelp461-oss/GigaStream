FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I reset my password?
Go to the login page and click "Forgot Password?" Enter your email and we'll send a reset link.
What payment methods are supported?
We support USDT BEP20 only. Minimum withdrawal is $10.00 USDT. A 2% network fee is deducted.
How does the CPM system work?
Earnings = (Valid Views / 1000) × CPM Rate that was set at the time the video/link was uploaded. Changing CPM only affects NEW uploads — old videos/links keep their original CPM rate locked in.
Does GigaStream store my files?
No. GigaStream does NOT store any files. Videos are hosted on voe.sx. We only store the URL, thumbnail, and analytics data.
What happens when I delete a video?
Only the app link is removed. The actual video on voe.sx is NOT deleted. Your earnings, views, and storage count are permanently preserved.
Why is my upload slow or failing?
Upload speed depends on your internet connection and file size. Large files (500MB+) can take 10–60 minutes. Do not close the page during upload.
How does referral commission work?
When your referred user makes a withdrawal and it is approved by admin, you automatically receive a commission percentage set by admin. The commission goes directly into your referral balance and is included in your total balance.
