Follow these steps to start earning with GigaStream:

1
Upload Your Link
Paste any external file URL (video, document, etc.) and generate a short GigaStream link.
2
Share Your Link
Share the generated Telegram link with your audience on any platform — Telegram, WhatsApp, etc.
3
Earn Per 1K Views
Get paid for every 1,000 valid views at the admin-set CPM rate. Minimum 200 views required to qualify.

If the CPM rate is $5.00 per 1,000 views, and your link gets 10,000 valid views, you earn:
10,000 ÷ 1,000 × $5.00 = $50.00

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions

How can I reset my password?
Go to the login page and click "Forgot Password?" Enter your email address and we'll send you a password reset link. Click the link in the email to set a new password.
How do I contact support?
Navigate to the Support page from the sidebar and click "Raise a Ticket." Fill in the subject and description of your issue, then submit. Our team will respond as soon as possible.
What payment methods are supported?
We support USDT BEP20 only. The minimum withdrawal amount is $10.00 USDT. A 2% network fee is deducted from your requested amount.
How does the CPM system work?
Each link uses the admin-set CPM rate. A link needs to reach the minimum valid views (set by admin) to qualify for earnings. Once qualified, earnings are calculated as: (Valid Views / 1000) × CPM Rate.
Does GigaStream store my files?
No. GigaStream does NOT store any files. You provide an external file URL, and we create a short monetized Telegram link. We only store the URL, thumbnail, and analytics data.
How does the referral commission work?
Share your referral link. When someone registers through your link and earns money, you receive a commission percentage (set by admin) of their earnings automatically. You do NOT earn just by inviting — commission is only earned when your referrals earn.
Why is my Telegram automatically logged in?
GigaStream uses your Telegram account's unique ID to identify you. If you have previously registered, opening the app on the same Telegram account will automatically log you in. Each Telegram account has its own separate GigaStream account.
